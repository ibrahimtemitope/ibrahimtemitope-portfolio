01. About Me

I'm Ibrahim Temitope (Kolade), a builder first. I started by helping my dad type documents as an accountant's son, taught myself desktop publishing on a scholarship in Lagos, then learned to code through freeCodeCamp, W3Schools and Proxy Coding School, Abeokuta. Most of what I know came from building while learning.

Frustrated by how freelance platforms bury new talent, I founded Tasknory LTD in 2025 — a CAC-registered marketplace that matches clients with vetted African AI specialists in 48 hours (not ratings, but skill verification). It’s now featured in Techpoint Africa, NaijaBreaking and Africa Startup Magazine.

Since then I’ve built AKHI (psychological red-teaming across 7 frontier AI models), GhostGrid (decentralized infra — 155k lines of Rust, 12 crates, architected solo with AI-assisted implementation), and Predator (autonomous pentest bot orchestrating 150+ Kali tools). I just entered FUNAAB for B.Sc. Computer Science (Sept 2026) and I’m looking for my first opportunity to grow in a real engineering team.
